This is Making Enemies

Making Enemies is a creative collective founded by Ayose Trujillo, focused on developing videogames and emotional experiences across multiple disciplines.

Born during game jams in 2020, where we created experimental titles within 48 hours, the project has evolved into a fully independent studio.

Today, that journey culminates with our first major title: Scholar Adventure: Mystery of Silence, now on Steam.

Scholar Adventure: Mystery of Silence

Mystery of Silence is our first game, a point-and-click adventure where William, a young writer in search of inspiration, enters an abbey shrouded in silence.
